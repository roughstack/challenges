# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive,
critique ideas rather than people, respect contributor privacy and security
embargoes, and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, or publication of private security information is unacceptable.

Report ordinary moderation concerns through a private maintainer channel and
security-sensitive concerns through the process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers
may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community and the
project.
